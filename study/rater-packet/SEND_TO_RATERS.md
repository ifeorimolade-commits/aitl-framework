# Sending the Rater Packet (for the author, not the raters)

## Who to ask

Two people with professional accessibility or UX evaluation experience who did not help build AIAAF. Co-authors and close collaborators are not the strongest choice, because their independence will be questioned. Someone who works in accessibility at a company or institution you have no reporting relationship with is ideal.

## What to send

After Step 3 of the runbook (raw corpus committed):

1. Make a folder called `AITL-Study1-Rater-Packet` containing:
   * `RATER_INSTRUCTIONS.md` and `../CODEBOOK.md`
   * `rater_patterns.csv`, `rater_scores.csv`, `rater_unclassified.csv`
   * `R1.html`, a copy of `corpus/source/C2-GP-A.html`
   * `R3.html`, a copy of `corpus/source/C1-CL-A.html`
   * `R2-link.txt`, containing only the published link for C3-FM-A
2. Zip it and send it by email with the message below.
3. When the sheets come back, save them as `data/coding_rater1.csv`, `data/scores_rater1.csv` (and `rater2`), add their unclassified rows to `data/unclassified_defects.csv` with a note of who found them, and save their emailed answers as `data/rater1_feedback.md`. Then rerun `summarize.py`.

## Message

> Subject: Request: 90 minute independent accessibility evaluation for an open study
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I am running a small open study on the accessibility of interfaces generated by AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT and Figma Make), using an evaluation framework I published this year, AIAAF, and the tool that applies it, AITL. I need two independent evaluators with accessibility experience to code three of the generated interfaces using the study's codebook, so I can check whether the framework gives consistent results in someone else's hands.
>
> It would take about 90 minutes: a keyboard pass and a screen reader pass on each interface, then filling in a short sheet. There are also six short feedback questions, and honest criticism is the most useful answer. Everything is in the attached packet, and the instructions explain how to do it.
>
> Your sheets would be published as part of the open study data, under your name only if you agree. I should also tell you how I may use the study beyond publishing it, so you can decide with that in mind: [author to fill in before sending].
>
> If you can do it, could you send the sheets back by [date]? If you can't, no problem at all, and thank you for reading.
>
> Thanks,
> Ife
